### Theory

The Divide and Conquer strategy is used to find the maximum and minimum elements in an array efficiently.

1. The array is divided into two halves.
2. The middle index is calculated to divide the array.
3. Each half is recursively divided until a single or two elements remain.
4. If there is one element, it is both the minimum and maximum.
5. If there are two elements, they are compared to find the minimum and maximum.
6. The minimum and maximum of both halves are compared separately.
7. The results are combined to obtain the overall minimum and maximum.
8. The process continues until the entire array is processed.

**Time Complexity:** O(n)

**Space Complexity:** O(log n) for the recursion stack.

**Conclusion:** The Divide and Conquer strategy efficiently finds the maximum and minimum elements in an array.
